# Figure 4

This directory contains the processed input data and custom scripts used to reproduce the competitive-fitness analyses and plots shown in Figure 4.

## Panels

- Figure 4a: Relationship between the competitive fitness of haploid segregants and their corresponding diploid or higher-ploidy derivatives.
- Figure 4b: Competitive-fitness trajectories of haploid-founded populations during experimental evolution.
- Figure 4c: Competitive-fitness trajectories of diploid- and higher-ploidy-founded populations during experimental evolution.

## Directory structure

- `data/`: grouped fitness estimates and evolved-population measurements
- `scripts/`: statistical-analysis and plotting scripts
- `output/`: reproducible summary tables and plots

Generation-0 grouped selection coefficients were estimated from independent biological competition assays. Final panel assembly was performed in Adobe Illustrator.
